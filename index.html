IMPORTANT SAFETY INFORMATION

What is SPRAVATO®?

SPRAVATO® (esketamine) is a prescription nasal spray and Schedule III controlled substance. In the U.S., it is approved for:

  • treatment-resistant depression in adults, either alone or with an oral antidepressant; and
  • depressive symptoms in adults with major depressive disorder with acute suicidal thoughts or behavior, together with an oral antidepressant.

It is self-administered only under a healthcare provider’s supervision in a certified treatment setting. Patients are monitored for at least 2 hours after each dose.

Important risks

SPRAVATO® has boxed warnings for sedation, dissociation, respiratory depression, abuse and misuse, and suicidal thoughts and behaviors. It is available only through the restricted SPRAVATO® REMS program.

Its effectiveness in preventing suicide or reducing suicidal thoughts or behavior has not been demonstrated, and it does not replace hospitalization when clinically warranted.
